# Steps-Till-Dawn
This is a 2D pixel RPG game about love, lost, and letting go.  
In this story, you need to find a way to bring your closest person back to life. But every step of you may affect your fate, and Every decision you choose may lead to a completely different ending.  

The game includes different endings, collect all of them to unlock the hidden room :) (originally planned to show one more story but Im lowkey running out of time so let's just do a room so I won't die before actually handing in the assignment.)

## Game Control
- `arrows` : Move
- `Esc`: Save game
- `i`: Open inventory
